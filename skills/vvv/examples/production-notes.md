# Worked production note: a wedding photo reel

This is a fictional planning example, not a completed project or evidence of a render. Paths, times and copy illustrate the record shape. Adapt the decisions to the actual task; do not claim the files or checks below exist.

## Brief and direction

An 18-second, 9:16 wedding reel for a phone feed. Start with the couple's first photograph, move through a ceremony detail and a family portrait, then end on their names and date. The chosen delivery is silent for autoplay; on-screen copy must carry the meaning. Keep the same couple visible through each transition. Use warm paper, one accent color and readable text.
